ImgBB is a free image hosting service. To let this tool upload your screenshots directly to ImgBB and generate quick links, a free API key is needed.

1
Create a free account on ImgBB
Go to imgbb.com and sign up for a free account (or log in if you already have one).
2
Get your free API Key
Visit api.imgbb.com and click the blue "Get API key" button to generate your 32-character key.
3
