⚙ Application Settings
👤 Character Profile
Store your character's in-game name. When your character is speaking, all lows and lowers appear white (matching your in-game perspective), while speech from other characters appears in muted tones. In phone conversations, your character speaks in natural tones while the other caller appears in phone yellow.
🎵 Background Music Player
Paste any YouTube song or video link (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=... or https://youtu.be/...). The 🎵 button in the header will play this track on repeat. This setting is saved permanently and is preserved even when resetting other configurations.
↺ Reset Settings
Restore all canvas sizing, typography, outline styles, background toggles, atmospheric filters, and censorship parameters back to their original defaults.
